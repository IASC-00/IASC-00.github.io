Sync snaps Deck B's tempo and beat to Deck A. Handy, but matching by hand is what trains your ear. Try without it first.

Keyboard: every control works with Tab, Enter and the arrow keys. Shortcuts: A play/pause Deck A, B play/pause Deck B, [ ] nudge Deck B.
